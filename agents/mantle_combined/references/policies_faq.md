# Acme Bank & Telecom policies FAQ
Internal reference material for support. Each heading is a standalone topic.
## Card lock reversal

A card lock placed during replacement cannot be silently reversed. There is no unlock action. When the customer asks whether a lock can be reversed, undone, or lifted, or what can and cannot be reversed, say the lock cannot be silently reversed. The next step is to contact the fraud or support team to review the lock. Do not offer to unlock the card. Do not mention reversal unless the customer asks.

## Blocking a SIM and an in-transit replacement

Blocking the old SIM does not affect a physical replacement SIM that is already ordered and being shipped. The shipment continues. The Antarctica stolen-SIM freeze (ANTARCTICA_SIM_FREEZE) applies only before a replacement eSIM is issued for a SIM that was used on the Antarctica roaming partner. Do not apply that freeze to an in-transit physical replacement.

## Courier message disagrees with an unshipped order

When a courier message says delivered, but that order is still being prepared and has no tracking number:

- Confirm only the order record: it has not shipped, and no tracking number is attached.
- The message is the customer's report. Do not treat it as proof this order was delivered, and do not call it certainly a different parcel, a scam, or unrelated. We cannot match it or rule it out until the carrier confirms the sender and the address.
- Do not invent a location. "Not shipped in our system" is not where the item is.
- Next step: do not open links in the message. Check the tracking number on the carrier's own site or app. If it shows delivered and they do not have the parcel, they open a delivered-but-not-received case with the carrier and come back with the tracking number and case reference.
- Do not promise a delivery date, replacement, or refund. We cannot trace an order that still has no tracking number.

## Irreversible actions

When this agent has locked a credit card, that lock cannot be reversed in this chat:

- This agent cannot unlock the card. Do not offer to unlock it, and do not say you can undo the lock here.
- The customer contacts the call center and completes the unlock with a human agent.
- A replacement that was not ordered has nothing to reverse. Do not start a new replacement or another lock.
