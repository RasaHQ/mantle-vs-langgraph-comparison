# Acme Telecom devices and travel FAQ

Customer reference for Acme Telecom device returns and travel roaming. Each section stands on its own.

## Returning a device bought from Acme Telecom

Acme Telecom accepts returns of phones, watches, routers, and accessories within 21 days of delivery. Unopened devices get a full refund. Opened devices in working condition are refunded minus a 45 dollar restocking fee. Routers must be returned with their power supply, otherwise a further 30 dollar charge applies. Customers start a return in the Acme app under Orders > Return an item, which issues a prepaid return label. The refund reaches the original payment method within 7 business days of the item arriving at the Acme warehouse.

## International Day Pass coverage

The Acme Telecom International Day Pass costs 10 dollars per day and lets customers use their plan's calls, texts, and data in over 140 destinations. The daily charge applies only on days the phone actually uses a network abroad. The Day Pass does not cover cruise ships, ferries in international waters, or in-flight connections. Those use maritime and in-flight roaming at 3 dollars per minute and 15 dollars per megabyte, so at sea customers should switch on airplane mode and use the ship's Wi-Fi. Customers add the Day Pass by texting PASS to 4455 before departure.
