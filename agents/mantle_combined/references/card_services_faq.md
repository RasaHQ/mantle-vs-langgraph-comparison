# Acme Bank card services FAQ

Customer reference for Acme Bank credit card services. Each section stands on its own.

## Recurring payments after a card replacement (Acme Card Updater)

When Acme Bank replaces a lost, stolen, or damaged credit card, Acme Card Updater automatically sends the new card number and expiry date to enrolled merchants. Subscriptions such as streaming services, phone plans, and gym memberships usually switch to the new card within 5 business days of the replacement being ordered, with no action from the customer. Merchants that are not enrolled in Card Updater keep the old card details, so the customer must update the card with those merchants directly. Card Updater is free and is switched on for every Acme Bank credit card.

## Disputing a credit card charge

Acme Bank customers can dispute a credit card charge within 120 days of the transaction date, in the Acme app under Card > Transactions > Dispute, or by phone. Acme Bank issues a provisional credit for the disputed amount within 2 business days of receiving the dispute. The investigation closes within 30 calendar days, and the customer receives the outcome by email. If the merchant's evidence is upheld, the provisional credit is removed on the next statement.
