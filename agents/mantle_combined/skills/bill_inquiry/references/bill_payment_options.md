# Acme Telecom mobile bill payment options

Customer reference for paying an Acme Telecom mobile bill. Each section stands on its own.

## Payment arrangements for a mobile bill

Acme Telecom customers who cannot pay a mobile bill in full by its due date can set up a payment arrangement. The bill is split into up to 3 monthly instalments with no extra fee, and the line stays active as long as each instalment is paid on time. A customer can have one payment arrangement in any 12-month period. Arrangements are set up in the Acme app under Billing > Payment arrangement at least 2 days before the due date.

## Late payment of a mobile bill

If an Acme Telecom mobile bill is still unpaid 10 days after its due date, a late payment fee of 8.50 dollars is added to the next bill. Outgoing calls and mobile data are restricted after 30 days of non-payment until the balance is paid.
