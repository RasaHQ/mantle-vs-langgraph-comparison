# Acme Telecom phone number and voicemail after a SIM block

Customer reference for what happens to an Acme Telecom mobile number while a lost or stolen SIM is blocked.

## Phone number and voicemail after a SIM is blocked

When Acme Telecom blocks a lost or stolen SIM, the customer's mobile number is reserved for 90 days so it can move to a replacement SIM or eSIM. The replacement keeps the same number, plan, and remaining data allowance. Voicemails stored on the Acme network are kept for 30 days after the block and can be played back in the Acme app under Mobile > Voicemail before the replacement is activated. Text messages sent to the number while the SIM is blocked are not delivered and are not stored.
